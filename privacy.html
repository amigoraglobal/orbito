Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

Orbito is a free app for random voice and video chat with new people. This page explains what information the app handles and why. Orbito is for adults only (18+).

What Orbito does not do

  • Orbito does not record, save or listen to your video or audio calls.
  • Orbito does not ask for your name, phone number, email address or password.
  • Orbito does not use advertising or analytics trackers in this version.

Information the app handles

  • Anonymous ID. When you open the app, it signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID that is not linked to your name or email.
  • Matching information. To pair you with someone, the app writes your anonymous ID, a timestamp, the chat type (video or voice) and the gender options you chose (who you are, who you want to meet) to a waiting list. Your entry is removed when you are matched, leave the queue or close the app.
  • Call set-up data. The two devices exchange technical connection details (such as network addresses and session descriptions) through Firebase so that a direct call can start. This data is deleted after the call ends where possible.
  • Camera and microphone. The app asks for permission to use them. Your video and audio are sent directly to the other person (peer-to-peer) and are not stored by Orbito.
  • Reports. If you report someone, the app stores your anonymous ID, the other person's anonymous ID, the chat type and the time. We use this to keep the community safe.
  • On your device. The app saves your choices (age confirmation, gender options, the list of people you blocked and demo settings) in your browser's local storage. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Why we use this information

  • To find you someone to talk to and set up the call.
  • To review reports and stop abuse.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

Who can see it

  • The person you talk to can see and hear what you share on the call. Because calls are direct, their device can also see your network (IP) address. They could also record the call on their own device, so only share what you are comfortable with.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) processes the matching, set-up and report data for us. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles data.
  • Google STUN servers help devices find each other and see your network address while doing so.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may keep standard server logs.

We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

  • Waiting-list entries and call set-up data are short-lived and removed when no longer needed.
  • Reports are kept for as long as needed to keep the community safe.
  • Information in your browser stays until you clear it.

Your choices

  • You can deny camera or microphone access in your browser, but calls will not work.
  • You can clear the app's saved data in your browser settings.
  • You can block and report anyone during a chat.
  • You can ask us questions or ask for your report data to be reviewed using the contact link below.

Children

Orbito is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly allow minors to use the app. If you believe a minor is using Orbito, please report that person in the app and contact us. We will act on it.

Security

We use Firebase security rules and encrypted connections (HTTPS, and encrypted WebRTC media). No online service is perfectly secure, so please take care when talking to strangers.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app grows (for example, when ads or paid plans are added). The effective date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue at github.com/amigoraglobal/orbito/issues.

This policy describes the current version of Orbito, an early test release.
